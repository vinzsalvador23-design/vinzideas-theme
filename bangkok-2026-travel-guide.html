Welcome to Bangkok: The City of Angels

Bangkok is one of Southeast Asia's most vibrant, chaotic, and exciting cities. Known for its ornate temples, delicious street food, thriving nightlife, and bustling markets, Bangkok offers incredible value for budget travelers. In 2026, Bangkok remains remarkably affordable compared to Western cities, with daily budgets as low as $25-35 for backpackers.

Why Visit Bangkok in 2026?

  • Incredible Street Food: World's best street food at unbeatable prices ($0.50-2 per dish)
  • Budget Accommodation: Dorm beds as low as $7-10/night in great locations
  • Ancient Temples: Thousands of ornate Buddhist temples, many free to explore
  • Vibrant Nightlife: Sky bars, night markets, go-go bars, club scene
  • Shopping Paradise: Floating markets, weekend markets, malls with endless options
  • Cheap Transportation: Taxis, tuktuks, BTS train at fraction of Western prices
Quick Facts:
  • Location: Capital of Thailand
  • Currency: Thai Baht (THB) - 1 USD ≈ 33-35 THB (2026 rates)
  • Average Daily Budget: $25-35 (backpacker), $60-100 (mid-range), $150+ (luxury)
  • Languages: Thai, some English in tourist areas
  • Best Time: November-February (cool & dry)
  • Visa: 30 days visa-free for many nationalities

Getting to Bangkok

By Air

Suvarnabhumi International Airport (BKK) is Thailand's main airport. Budget airlines (AirAsia, Nok Air, Thai AirAsia) offer cheap flights throughout Southeast Asia.

Flight Costs (2026):

  • International flights: $300-700 (depending on origin)
  • Regional flights: $30-80 from nearby Southeast Asian cities
  • Airport to city: Airport bus $3, airport rail $3-4, taxi $12-25

By Bus/Train

Bangkok is a Southeast Asia hub. Buses from Chiang Mai, Phuket, Cambodia, Laos ($10-35), trains from northern Thailand ($5-15).

Perfect 4-Day Bangkok Itinerary

Temples, markets, street food, and nightlife. Estimated cost: $100-150 per person.

Day 1: Old Bangkok & Temples

Morning: Visit Grand Palace and Wat Phra Kaew ($15). Dress modestly (long pants/skirt, covered shoulders).

Afternoon: Wat Pho with reclining Buddha ($5). Walk across Chao Phraya River to explore local markets.

Evening: Street food dinner in Chinatown. Budget: $25-35

Day 2: Markets & Local Culture

Early Morning: Damnoen Saduak Floating Market (2-hour boat tour, $15-20). See vendors in traditional long-tail boats.

Afternoon: Rest or explore Jim Thompson's House museum ($10).

Evening: Muay Thai boxing match ($20-25) or local beer garden. Budget: $50-65

Day 3: Modern Bangkok & Shopping

Morning: Chatuchak Weekend Market (largest market in the world, free to explore). Browse 15,000+ stalls.

Afternoon: Mall of Bangkok or Emporium (air-conditioned shopping). Thai massage session ($6-8).

Evening: Rooftop bar with city views for drinks and sunset ($10-15). Budget: $35-50

Day 4: River & Departure

Morning: Chao Phraya River cruise ($5-10). Visit Wat Arun (Temple of Dawn) ($4).

Afternoon: Last-minute shopping at street markets or malls.

Evening: Departure or extend stay. Budget: $20-30

Alternative: 7-Day Deep Dive Bangkok
  • Days 1-4: Same as above
  • Day 5: Day trip to Ayutthaya ancient capital (2-hour train) or Khao Yai National Park
  • Day 6: Relaxation, spa day, street food crawl
  • Day 7: Pack shopping, goodbyes, depart
  • Estimated cost: $250-350 for 7 days

Money-Saving Tips & Essential Info

Food Savings

🍜 Eat Only Street Food: Street food vendors have the cheapest, most authentic food. Pad Thai, satay, mango sticky rice all under $2.
🍜 Markets for Meals: Floating markets and weekend markets have food stalls cheaper than restaurants.
🍜 Breakfast Special: Jok (rice porridge) or dosas for $1-2 are perfect cheap breakfasts.

Transportation Hacks

🚇 Insist on Meter in Taxis: Always ask taxi drivers to use the meter. Pre-agreed prices are scams.
🚇 Use BTS & MRT: Fastest and cheapest way around Bangkok. Modern, clean, English signage.
🚇 Tuk-Tuks for Short Distances: Tuk-tuks fun but negotiate price first (no meter). For longer distances, use taxi with meter.

Accommodation Tips

🏨 Khao San Road is Cliché But Cheap: Famous backpacker street has best prices and social atmosphere.
🏨 Dorms are Social: Great way to meet other travelers. Most Bangkok hostels offer group activities and tours.
🏨 Airbnb Private Rooms: Sometimes cheaper than hotels in same area ($20-35/night).

Tourist Scams to Avoid

⚠️ Closed Temple Scam: Tuk-tuk drivers claim temples are closed, offer alternate tours. It's a scam. Go directly to temples!
⚠️ Gem Shop Scam: Avoid tuk-tuk recommendations for gem shops. Tourist ripoff.
⚠️ Drink Spiking: Be careful accepting drinks from strangers. Stay in groups at night.

Money Tips

💵 ATM Withdrawals: Bangkok has ATMs everywhere. Thai ATMs charge $2-3 fee but worth it for better exchange rates.
💵 Currency Exchange: Exchange money at banks or money changers in shopping malls, not at airport (bad rates).

Cultural Tips

🙏 Respect the Royals: Never disrespect the Thai royal family. Images on currency must be respected.
🙏 Temple Dress Code: Wear long pants/skirt and covered shoulders when visiting temples.
🙏 Shoe Removal: Always remove shoes when entering temples and many homes/shops.
